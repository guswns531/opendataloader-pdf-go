# Types of Sources

I  am  a  big  fan  of  Mike  CaulfieldÕs  information  literacy  work  (see the  next  chapter,  SIFTing  Information.)  Sometimes  I  have  found my  attempts  to  use  his  strategies  in  the  classroom  were  hard  for

students.  For  example,  when  IÕve  tried  the  exercise  about  the American  Academy  of  Pediatrics  and  the  American  College  of Pediatricians  (Reflection  &  Discussion  Question  1)  without  first talking  about  professional  organizations,  students  rarely  got  how they were different, and it did not build their confidence. ItÕs hard to identify a legitimate professional association if youÕve never  heard  of  the  concept  of  professional  associations.  This chapter  may  be  long,  but  I  felt  it  was  important  to  enumerate  at least some of the dimensions of the sources they may find, so that when we get to CaulfieldÕs SIFT method they are set up for success.
