The R eynolds number is a dimensionless parameter that is the ratio of the inertial (destabilizing) force to the viscosity (stabilizing) force. As Re increases, the inertial force becomes relatively larger, and the flow destabilizes and becomes fully turbulent.

The R eynolds experiment  determines  the critical Reynolds number for pipe flow at which laminar flow  (Re<2000 )  becomes  transitional  (2000<R e<4000 )  and  the  transitional  flow  becomes  turbulent (Re>4000). The advantage of using a critical Reynolds number , instead of critical velocity, is that the results of the experiments are applicable to all Newtonian fluid flow s in pipes with a circular cross- section.
