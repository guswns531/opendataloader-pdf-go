  1. The field of view becomes darker

  2. The size of the image increases

  3. The resolution (ability to see detail) increases

  4. The working distance between the slide and the objective lens decreases

  5. The depth of focus (thickness of the specimen that is visible) is reduced

  6. When changing from scanning to low power the field of view gets smaller. In fact, every time you increase the power of the objective, the field gets smaller. Steps for Using the Microscope:

  7. Place the slide on the stage lining it up with the rectangle and using the stage clip to hold it in place.

  8. Click the nosepiece to the lowest (shortest) setting, thescanning objective lens or 4x.

  9. Look into the eyepiece.

  10. Use the coarse adjustment knobto bring the specimen into view. The specimen must be in focus before moving to the next steps.

  11. Rotate the nosepiece to the -powerlowobjective or 10x.

  12. Refocus using the coarse adjustment knob.

  13. Move the slide to get a centered view.

  14. Now use the fine adjustment knob to get the specimen in perfect focus.

  15. Your slide MUST be focused on low power before attempting this next step.
