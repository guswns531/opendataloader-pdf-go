Under the FIT scheme, biomass fuels for power generation are grouped into six categories. x General wood: sawmill residues, import wood such as pellets and chips, palm kernel shell (PKS) and palm trunk x Liquid biomass: palm oil x Unutilised wood: domestic thinned wood x Construction wood waste: wood waste salvaged from construction and other wood materials x Waste materials and other biomass: pruned branched, paper, food waste, waste cooking oil, and black liquor x Biogas: methane derived from sewage sludge, manure, and food waste. While  inexpensive  biomass  sources  such  as  wood  waste  from  construction  and  waste materials,  were  the  main  fuels  under  the  RPS,  the  domestic  unutilised  wood  and  the general wood whose tariff rates are set higher increased specifically (Figure 4.1, 4.2).
